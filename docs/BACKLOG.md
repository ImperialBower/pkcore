the crate's own gate settings (`#![warn(clippy::pedantic, unwrap_used,
expect_used)]` plus a `todo!()` ban in `clippy.toml`). The remaining panic

decision before it is a task — see the entry in the debt doc.

`Table` rewrite, one misfiled by the 2026-07 rename, one justified), and three

re-raised next pass.
