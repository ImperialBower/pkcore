# AI Notes: World Changes

There was this one dev that I used to work with that was one of the
most tallented members of the team. He liked to work late, and he liked
to refactor things. He didn't do it very often. Most of the time he would just work as your 
every day dev. But then, when the moon was fool, the World Changer would come out. 

Everyone else would leave the office, bidding him goodbye as he pounded away at his
workstation, lazer focused on his task. We would all arrive bright and early the next
morning, only to discover a pull request with almost every file in the application
changed one way or another. 

Everyone else on the team would need to drop everything, and spend all day trying to
decipher what he had done. You could ask him why he did one of the hundreds of things
that he did, and he was always have a good answer. Unfortunately, almost none of them
had anything to do with the actual assignment he was working on.
