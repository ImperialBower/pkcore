# Data Assets

Concepts bound to the datasets shipped in `data/` (curated, committed inputs)
and produced in `generated/` (default runtime output directory — session YAML,
`hups.db`, `hups.bin`, `bcm.zst`).

* [HUP equity databases](hup-databases.md) - The heads-up preflop SQLite family, its schema, and the regeneration pipeline.
* [Bot profiles](bot-profiles.md) - BotProfile YAML personalities in data/bots/ and the trained exploit config.
* [Hand histories](hand-histories.md) - Session YAML records in data/hands/ used for replay and defect reports.
* [Pluribus logs](pluribus-logs.md) - Raw and converted hand logs from the Pluribus research bot.
